# Cover Letter

Used when an application REQUIRES a cover letter (text box or file upload; uploads are
rendered to a simple PDF). Optional cover letters are skipped unless
settings.yaml answers.cover_letter_when_optional is true.

## Your guidance
Replace this section with your preferred structure and tone, e.g.:
- 3 short paragraphs, under 300 words.
- Paragraph 1: the role and one specific, researched reason this company (product, mission, engineering work).
- Paragraph 2: the most relevant real project/experience from profile/profile.md, with a concrete result.
- Paragraph 3: what you want to learn/contribute; thank them.
Include a sample letter based on your real experience if you have one — it is treated as
style guidance, not pasted verbatim. Never add facts that are not in your profile.
