# Candidate narrative source of truth

This file is a blank factual worksheet. Replace guidance with your real details.
Do not invent achievements to complete a section. Mark unknown details explicitly.
The structured facts in `config/candidate.yaml` should agree with this narrative.
If two sources disagree, correct them before enabling submission.

## Education
Record institutions, degree names, majors/minors, enrollment dates, graduation date,
coursework, GPA and scale if you want to disclose them. Include supporting context.

## Employment
For each employer: exact title, dates, location, responsibilities, tools, and factual
outcomes. Separate paid employment from volunteering and personal projects.

## Projects and technical experience
For each real project: purpose, your personal contribution, tools, team size,
challenges, decisions, results, and URLs. Identify measured versus estimated results.

## Leadership, teaching, and extracurricular activities
Describe what you personally did, with dates and concrete examples.

## Accomplishments and interests
Use genuine accomplishments and interests that can support relevant essays.

## Story bank
For teamwork, conflict, failure, leadership, and technical challenges, record the
situation, your actions, the outcome, and what you learned. Avoid sensitive third
party details. Templates can refer to these stories but cannot create new facts.
